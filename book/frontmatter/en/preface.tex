\section*{Acknowledgements}

This book would not have been possible without the people who have been
by my side since long before its first page existed.

To my father and my mother, for their unwavering support and for always
providing me with the means to keep learning, without ever asking what
for. This book is, to a large extent, the fruit of that trust.

To my lifelong friends, who have always been there. They have had the
patience to hear about groups, graphs and manifolds more often than
anyone should, and the generosity to stay when studying took time away
from everything else.

To the artificial intelligence models that accompanied me throughout the
writing. Their help was not confined to this book: they were patient and
tireless study companions during the years in which I learned
mathematics from home while holding down a job, answering questions at
any hour and pointing out mistakes that would otherwise have gone
unnoticed. Any that remain are mine alone.
